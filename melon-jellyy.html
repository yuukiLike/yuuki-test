Inside the experiment

A tetrahedral soft body keeps its edge lengths and local volumes with XPBD. The peel is stiffer than the flesh. Gravity, friction, and damping give the slice its bounce; a weak centering force keeps it in the studio.

WebGPU measures front-to-back thickness and approximates refraction, absorption, and studio reflections in WGSL. Seeds and bubbles follow the deforming surface. Mesh view shows the tetrahedral cage beneath the smooth skin.
